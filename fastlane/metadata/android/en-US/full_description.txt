liftmath is a set of gym calculators: 1RM, plate loading, strength scores, and world records.

One-rep max. Enter a weight and rep count, and it runs six rep-max formulas at once, then shows their median as a consensus estimate.

Plate loading works out which plates go on the bar and draws the barbell. Standard bar, women's bar, or your own plate inventory, it handles all three.

Strength scores: Wilks, DOTS, IPF GL.

World records covers powerlifting, strongman, and grip. Track and field too, at world, college, and high school level.

A plain lb/kg converter is in there as well.

The app has no internet permission, so it never makes a network request. There's no account and no ads, and it doesn't track you. A saved plate setup or theme choice stays in local storage on your phone, nowhere else.

liftmath is free and open source software.
